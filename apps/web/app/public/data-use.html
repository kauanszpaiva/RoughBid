RoughBid Data Use and AI Policy

Operational draft. This page explains intended handling for construction plans, project data, and AI-assisted review.

Plan Files

Plans and drawings may contain confidential project, client, property, and contractor information. RoughBid should store plan files privately and restrict access by organization role.

AI Suggestions

AI-generated scope, quantities, missing-trade flags, and explanations are suggestions. The estimator must review and approve every item before it becomes part of an estimate or client proposal.

Human Review Required

  • Confirm measurements against the latest plan revision.
  • Confirm unit costs, labor assumptions, overhead, markup, and margin.
  • Confirm exclusions, alternates, taxes, permits, and local requirements.
  • Review client-facing proposals before sharing.

Data Minimization

Only upload files and enter project details needed for estimating. Avoid unnecessary personal, payment, legal, medical, or unrelated sensitive data.
