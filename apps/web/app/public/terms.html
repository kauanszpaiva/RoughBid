RoughBid Terms of Use

Operational draft. These terms are a product baseline and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before broad commercial use.

Use of RoughBid

RoughBid is an independent SaaS product for construction estimating, takeoff organization, project review, and proposal exports. You are responsible for verifying project scope, quantities, pricing, taxes, permits, code requirements, and final contract language before relying on any output.

Accounts and Organizations

Organization administrators control teammate invitations and roles. Users must keep access links, sessions, and account credentials secure and may only access workspaces they are authorized to use.

AI and Plan Review

Any AI-assisted plan reading or scope suggestions are review aids. They do not replace professional judgment, licensed contractor review, engineering review, or code compliance review. Suggested quantities and scope items must be confirmed by a human before use.

Customer Content

You retain responsibility for plans, drawings, estimates, client details, files, and other content uploaded or entered into RoughBid. Do not upload content unless you have the right to process it in the product.

Prohibited Use

  • No unauthorized access, credential sharing, scraping, abuse, or security testing outside approved scopes.
  • No use of RoughBid outputs as final legal, tax, engineering, architectural, or financial advice.
  • No uploading malware, unlawful content, or data you are not permitted to process.

Availability and Changes

RoughBid may change as the product matures. Features may be beta, limited, or unavailable while integrations are configured and validated.
