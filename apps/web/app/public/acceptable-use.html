RoughBid Acceptable Use Policy

Operational draft. This policy should be reviewed before broad commercial use.

Allowed Use

Use RoughBid to organize construction estimating work, review plans, manage project scope, calculate estimates, and export internal or client-facing estimate materials.

Disallowed Use

  • Do not attempt unauthorized access to other organizations, projects, files, accounts, or infrastructure.
  • Do not upload malware, exploit payloads, unlawful content, or unrelated sensitive personal data.
  • Do not use AI suggestions as final professional advice without qualified review.
  • Do not stress test, scrape, reverse engineer, or abuse the service outside written authorization.

Security Reports
