RoughBid Acceptable Use Policy

Operational draft. This policy is not legal advice and should be reviewed before broad commercial use.

1. Allowed Use

Use RoughBid to organize construction estimating work, review plans, manage project scope, calculate estimates, manage project-specific price assumptions, share proposals, and support client acceptance workflows.

2. Account and Organization Boundaries

  • Do not access or attempt to access another organization, project, file, proposal, price list, billing record, or user account unless authorized.
  • Do not forward login magic links, invite links, private file links, or internal workspace links to unauthorized people.
  • Do not use shared accounts unless the organization has explicitly approved that workflow.

3. Disallowed Content and Activity

  • Do not upload malware, exploit payloads, unlawful content, or content that infringes third-party rights.
  • Do not upload plans, drawings, datasets, price tables, photos, or client information unless you have the right to process them in RoughBid.
  • Do not upload unnecessary payment card data, government identifiers, health data, biometric data, children's data, passwords, API keys, or unrelated sensitive personal data.
  • Do not scrape, crawl, reverse engineer, stress test, benchmark-abuse, or attack the service outside written authorization.
  • Do not bypass usage limits, credit limits, billing controls, file size limits, AI rate limits, or marketplace entitlements.
  • Do not use RoughBid outputs as final legal, tax, accounting, engineering, architectural, permitting, or licensed construction advice.

4. AI and Pricing Abuse

Do not use RoughBid to generate deceptive bids, fraudulent proposals, unsafe construction instructions, fake signatures, unauthorized price datasets, or outputs intended to mislead clients, inspectors, insurers, lenders, or public authorities.

5. Client Proposal Links

Proposal links are intended for the named client or authorized recipient. Recipients should not alter, forward, scrape, or misuse proposal pages. Opening or signing a proposal may create an audit event and notify the sender.

6. Enforcement

RoughBid may limit, suspend, or remove access where activity creates security risk, legal risk, billing risk, service abuse, or risk to another customer.

7. Security Reports

Report security concerns to hello@kspdominion.group.
