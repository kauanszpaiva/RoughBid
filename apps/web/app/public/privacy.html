RoughBid Privacy Policy

Operational draft. This policy documents intended product behavior and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before broad commercial use.

Data We Process

RoughBid may process account email addresses, organization membership, project names, client/project details, plan files, takeoff quantities, estimate line items, pricing assumptions, proposal exports, and security logs.

How Data Is Used

Data is used to provide estimating workflows, authenticate users, enforce workspace access, generate exports, troubleshoot issues, improve product reliability, and maintain security.

AI Plan Reading

When AI plan reading is enabled, uploaded plans and related project context may be processed to extract candidate scope, quantities, notes, and review flags. AI output should be treated as a draft suggestion until confirmed by a user.

Sharing and Processors

RoughBid may rely on infrastructure and product processors such as hosting, database, authentication, storage, email, payment, logging, and AI providers. Production processor configuration must match the environment actually deployed.

Security

RoughBid uses workspace roles, row-level security, private plan storage, hashed invite tokens, and server-only credentials for sensitive integrations. Users should report suspected unauthorized access quickly.

Retention

Project and account data may be retained while an organization account is active and for a reasonable period needed for security, audit, dispute, legal, or backup purposes.
