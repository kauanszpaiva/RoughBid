01 THE PRODUCT

ONE ESTIMATING SYSTEM

Not another dashboard full of AI buttons.

RoughBid is built around the estimating job itself. The software keeps the source drawings, human-reviewed findings, quantities, costs and proposal output connected from start to finish.

PLANS

Keep the source of truth close.

Upload plan sets, preserve revisions and work from the drawings that the estimate actually came from.

REVIEW

Nothing counts until you approve it.

Plan-reading assistance can surface possible scope and quantities, but findings stay reviewable before entering the estimate.

ESTIMATE

See the number being built.

Separate materials, labor and equipment. Apply overhead and markup with a clear view of the final client price.

DELIVERY

Keep internal math internal.

Create client-ready proposals and links without exposing the cost detail you use to run the job.
